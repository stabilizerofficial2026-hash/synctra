HELP CENTER

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SyncTrA?

A private creative and social community for sharing, discovering interests and connecting with members.

Can I join different belief and philosophy discussions?

Yes. Open Minds includes separate discussion spaces for different beliefs, science, philosophy and technology. Joining optional rooms is your choice.

How do I find another member?

Use Explore & Network or open a member's name or profile photo from a community post.

What is CTY?

CTY is an internal platform credit. It is not cash, cannot be withdrawn or traded, and has no guaranteed monetary value.

How do memberships work?

Membership benefits and prices are shown on the Membership page. Checkout is not available until a secure payment provider is connected.

How can I report or block someone?

Use the report or block controls available on community content. Reports can be reviewed by authorized moderators.
